TRADE TECH
What it solves

One platform.
Two experiences.

For businesses, Trade Tech brings everyday management into a single mobile workflow. For customers, it offers a simple way to discover products and submit pre-orders to stores.

FlutterDartSupabaseAndroidWeb
01 / Business workspace

Operations in one place

  • Product and inventory management
  • Sales and expense recording
  • Customer pre-order management
  • Business reports and exports
02 / Customer workspace

Discover, order, follow up

  • Browse products from participating businesses
  • Add items to a shopping cart
  • Submit customer pre-orders
  • Track and manage order status (features under development)
